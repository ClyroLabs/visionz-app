## Proposed text

VisionZ Entertainment is a next-generation streaming and synthetic media platform by Clyro Labs, built on Solana.

**What are we building?**

Three pillars in one platform:

- **AI Smart Filter (live in prototype):** AI moderation of visuals and audio before publishing, plus kids mode and parental face check, so children only see age-appropriate titles.
- **Synthetic Media Studio (live in prototype):** Clyro Synth and Jukebox let users create animated films and music with AI, aiming to cut production costs sharply.
- **$VZN on Solana devnet (live today):** our only real on-chain path today. Users watch, complete a mission and receive $VZN on devnet. Mainnet comes next.

**Next steps (not live yet):** full token economy (deflationary design, creator royalties, subscription discounts), fiat↔$VZN conversion and the Launchpad presale. These run only as a sandbox demo today.

**Who is it for?**

- **Families and parents (B2C):** safer streaming with AI moderation and parental controls.
- **Creators and animators (prosumers):** accessible AI tools, direct payouts and fair content rights.
- **Schools and B2B:** licensing of our AI moderation through APIs (pilot stage).

## What changed and why

- **Devnet is the only real on-chain path:** stated explicitly.
- **Token economy, conversion and presale are next steps:** they're now marked as next steps, not current features.
- **Removed "guaranteed digital safety":** no moderation can guarantee safety, and judges may challenge it.
- **Removed "real-time frame-by-frame":** today moderation runs before publishing, not live.
- **Removed "90% reduced costs":** there's no source for it. "Aiming to cut costs sharply" is safe; add the number back only if you can back it up.
- **Removed "HD animated series":** changed to "films and music", which is what the prototype makes today.
- **Marked "Schools and B2B" as "pilot stage":** there are no licensing customers yet.
