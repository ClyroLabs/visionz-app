# Pitch deck: first-month acquisition and beta conversion test

The deck stays at 8 slides. Two slides change so judges see what we prove first and what comes after.

## Slide 7: "Forecast" becomes "Validate first, then forecast"
- The top half is new: **What we test in the closed beta**.
  - One conversion to test: **Free to paid (Premium/Family) within 30 days**.
  - Pass threshold: **at least 5% of active beta users** (the market reference is 2–5% for freemium streaming; this is a goal, not data).
  - Second signal: **at least 40% of families return in week 4**.

## Slide 8: Roadmap gets a "Month 1" block
- New card **Month 1 acquisition (small, hands-on)**:

  - **Family creators:** 10 family or children's content creators publishing with Synth/Jukebox. Goal: 20 titles published.
  - **Overall goal:** 100 active beta users by the end of month 1.
  - Channel: direct contact by the founders (visits, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs), with no paid ads.
- The roadmap shrinks to one line (MVP, then Beta, then Production, then Ecosystem) to make room.
- The 12-month goals now start from the beta result ("if conversion is at least 5%, then…").
